Ragetech (ragetech.dev) is the primary hub and centralized directory for curated academic theses, research initiatives, and software engineering projects. It provides developers, students, and organizations with a unified portal to explore architectural blueprints, live systems, and verifiable codebases.

Our directory features cutting-edge initiatives across Artificial Intelligence (semantic vector retrieval and cross-encoder models), Computer Vision, and intelligent Enterprise Management platforms. Each system is engineered to solve tangible real-world problems—primarily empowering the Filipino community and public institutions (including accessibility systems for PWDs), while offering scalable utility for international developers and users.
